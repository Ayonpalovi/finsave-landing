Questions? Answers.

FinSave is a money app for young adults aged 18–25 in the Netherlands, students first. It shows how much you can safely spend each day and helps you build savings goals and money habits.

It’s the amount you can spend today without touching rent, bills or your savings, so your money lasts until payday. In the demo you can see it on the dashboard next to your days to payday.

No. The demo needs no sign-up and runs on example data, so you can click around without entering anything about yourself.

No. Everything in the demo is example data. It’s there to show you how FinSave works, not to track your actual accounts.

When FinSave spots an unplanned purchase, you can wait 24 hours before the next one. It’s a small habit that helps you spend on purpose, without guilt.

No ads. The Deals section has no affiliate links either.
