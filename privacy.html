Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the FinSave website and demo handle, why, and what your rights are. FinSave is a student project made at Inholland University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands. We try to collect as little as possible.

1. Who is responsible

The FinSave student team is responsible for this website. You can reach us at hello@finsave.example.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address, if you give it to us. When you use “Get updates when FinSave launches”, we use your email only to tell you when FinSave launches. We don’t sell it or share it for marketing.
  • How you use the page, only if you allow it. See “Analytics” below.
  • Technical data our host needs. Like every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes basic request data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Nothing from the demo. The demo runs on example data. Don’t enter real bank details, passwords or other sensitive information into it.

3. Analytics (Microsoft Clarity) and cookies

If you click Allow in the analytics bar, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use this page: for example where they click, how far they scroll and where they get stuck. Clarity records these interactions anonymously; text you type into fields, such as your email, is hidden from recordings. Clarity uses cookies to recognise a returning browser. Microsoft processes this data for us; see Microsoft’s privacy statement.

If you click No thanks, Clarity is never loaded. You can change your choice at any time with “Analytics settings” at the bottom of the home page.

We don’t use advertising cookies and we show no ads. To load fonts and icons, your browser also connects to Google Fonts, jsDelivr and unpkg, which can see your IP address as part of that request.

4. Why we may use your data (legal basis)

We send launch updates and run analytics only with your consent, which you give by submitting your email or by clicking “Allow” in the analytics bar. We process technical data because we have a legitimate interest in running a working, secure website.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your email until FinSave launches and we have told you, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. Technical logs are kept by our host for a short, limited period.

6. Who else handles your data

Only the service providers we need to run the site: our hosting provider, Microsoft (Clarity analytics, only if you allow it), and the email or form service we use for launch updates. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (in Dutch: AVG) you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email us and we’ll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
